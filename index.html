A group led by Dr. Wenwu Jiang

  • We develop accurate and efficient machine-learning interatomic potentials for large-scale atomistic simulations.
  • We investigate phonon thermal transport in low-dimensional materials.
  • We study interfacial friction in van der Waals materials.
Research

Our research focuses on atomistic descriptions of coupled mechanical and thermal phenomena. We aim to establish predictive links between interatomic interactions, phonon transport, and interfacial motion by combining first-principles calculations, machine-learning potentials, and large-scale molecular dynamics simulations.

(1) Machine-learning interatomic potentials. We develop transferable and computationally efficient force fields trained on first-principles data.

(2) Thermal transport. We investigate phonon thermal transport in low-dimensional materials, including the effects of strain, defects, stacking, and moiré structures.

(3) Interfacial friction and mechanics. We study atomic-scale sliding, structural reconstruction, interfacial bonding, and the coupling between mechanical deformation and heat transport.
